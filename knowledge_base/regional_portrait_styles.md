# Photography Styles (現代人像攝影風格)

| 攝影風格 (Photography Style) | 靈感備註 (Inspiration Note) | 英文 Prompt 關鍵字 (Prompt Keywords) | 風格說明 (Style Description) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mika Ninagawa（蜷川實花） | 濃烈色彩與花卉戲劇感 | `Inspired by Mika Ninagawa, hyper-saturated theatrical portraiture with vivid reds, hot pinks and cyan highlights, floral excess, glossy artificial light, ornamental set-piece drama, shimmering maximalist fantasy, decadent pop surrealism` | 強烈飽和、鮮花、彩色光與夢境般張力，適合棚拍、花牆、彩色佈景與戲劇化寫真。 |
| Yoshihiko Ueda（上田義彥） | 靜默自然影像語言 | `Inspired by Yoshihiko Ueda, quiet natural portraiture with deep velvety shadows, cool desaturated greens and charcoals, meditative stillness, tactile large-format depth, low-key atmospheric elegance, restrained luminous air` | 深色陰影、低調光、冷淡色盤與靜默情緒，適合森林、窗邊、低光室內等安靜場景。 |
| Osamu Yokonami（橫浪修） | 群像秩序與安靜疏離感 | `Inspired by Osamu Yokonami, high-key minimalist portraiture with muted pastels, luminous whites, flat overcast softness, low contrast, orderly spacing, generous negative space, quiet distant atmosphere` | 強調排列、留白、安靜與輕微距離感，適合雙人構圖、空曠室內、校舍與極簡空間。 |
| Rinko Kawauchi（川內倫子） | 輕盈日常與微光語言 | `Inspired by Rinko Kawauchi, airy high-key photography with pale luminous softly overexposed daylight glow, cool shimmering pastel tones, fragile poetic everyday details, gentle domestic stillness, delicate haiku-like tenderness` | 輕盈、柔光、日常碎片與微過曝感，適合窗邊、居家、草地與安靜生活場景。 |
| Masumi Ishida（石田真澄） | 夏日底片感與柔亮彩色空氣 | `Inspired by Masumi Ishida, luminous youthful portraiture with gentle summer daylight, softly lifted exposure, airy pastel color balance, peach skin tones, washed film texture, translucent highlights, light cotton and sun-faded fabric atmosphere` | 著重柔亮自然光、微微提亮的曝光感、帶空氣感的 pastel 配色與淡淡底片質地，適合夏日街道、校園、窗邊、公園與清爽生活感畫面。 |
| Orie Ichihashi（市橋織江） | 透明自然光與低飽和靜謐色盤 | `Inspired by Orie Ichihashi, quiet natural portraiture with transparent window light, soft open shadows, low-saturation neutral palette, creamy whites, dusty blues and pale beige tones, delicate film grain, tactile linen and paper-like texture` | 著重透明窗光、柔和開放陰影、低飽和米白藍灰色盤與細緻底片顆粒，整體質感安靜克制，適合室內窗邊、旅館、公寓與日常靜物式人像。 |
| Yoko Takahashi（高橋ヨーコ） | 海風日光與乾爽褪色色調 | `Inspired by Yoko Takahashi, breezy coastal portraiture with crisp sunlit contrast, dry sun-bleached colors, faded blues, chalky whites, weathered skin texture, open sky brightness, salt-air clarity, relaxed editorial realism` | 著重海邊日光、乾爽高亮對比、褪色藍白色調與被陽光曬淡的質感，整體更鬆弛、通風、帶鹽分空氣感，適合海邊、天台、街道與開闊戶外場景。 |
| Paolo Roversi（保羅・羅韋爾西） | 柔霧高級時裝語言 | `Inspired by Paolo Roversi, soft haze couture portraiture with dim romantic light, velvety shadows, painterly softness, muted elegant tones, timeless dreamlike femininity, fragile old-world glamour` | 柔霧、低速感、暗色調與古典脆弱感，適合棚拍、復古室內與高級時裝畫面。 |
| Ellen von Unwerth（艾倫・馮・昂沃斯） | 俏皮性感與抓拍雜誌語言 | `Inspired by Ellen von Unwerth, playful sensual editorial portraiture with spontaneous energy, candid movement, flirtatious mischief, flash-assisted glamour, lively feminine confidence, mischievous magazine spontaneity` | 俏皮、性感、抓拍感強，介於雜誌與寫真之間，適合床景、沙發、街頭與隨拍主題。 |
| Nan Goldin（南・戈爾丁） | 私人相簿與粗粒子室內語言 | `Inspired by Nan Goldin, intimate lived-in portraiture with warm indoor light, heavy grain, raw emotional immediacy, messy domestic realism, private-room closeness, diaristic snapshot feeling` | 私密、粗粒子、暖光與生活混亂感，適合臥室、旅館、洗手台鏡前等私人空間。 |
| Juergen Teller（尤爾根・特勒） | 直閃粗糙與反精緻時裝語言 | `Inspired by Juergen Teller, raw direct-flash portraiture with anti-polished editorial tension, awkward realism, immediate camera presence, blunt luxury casualness, deliberately unvarnished fashion attitude` | 直打閃光、不完美與反精緻時尚感，適合白牆、電梯、街頭、試衣間與社群式大片。 |
| Richard Avedon（理察・阿維頓） | 純背景與強存在感語言 | `Inspired by Richard Avedon, stripped-down studio portraiture with a clean backdrop, crisp tonal contrast, direct visual authority, commanding presence, iconic fashion simplicity, razor-clear editorial focus` | 純背景、極簡、人物存在感極強，適合棚拍、封面感企劃與高辨識度人物主題。 |
| Alec Soth（亞歷克・索斯） | 寬鬆構圖與平靜敘事語言 | `Inspired by Alec Soth, spacious documentary portraiture with soft natural light, understated storytelling, calm emotional distance, open roadside stillness, subtle melancholy, quiet American wandering` | 構圖留白較多、氣氛平靜、帶有孤獨與旅行感，適合郊區、公路、旅館與空曠戶外場景。 |
| Sally Mann（莎莉・曼） | 古典濕版與記憶感影像語言 | `Inspired by Sally Mann, antique wet-plate portraiture with soft blur, ghostly texture, timeworn atmosphere, poetic decay, nostalgic stillness, haunted Southern memory` | 古典、朦朧、帶有時間感與衰敗氣息，適合森林、老屋、復古室內與低調黑白畫面。 |
| Wolfgang Tillmans（沃夫岡・提爾曼斯） | 生活切片與非正式影像語言 | `Inspired by Wolfgang Tillmans, casual everyday portraiture with snapshot energy, natural-light realism, spontaneous composition, contemporary lifestyle mood, relaxed documentary tone, intimate social immediacy` | 生活切片、輕鬆隨拍與非正式構圖，適合公寓、咖啡店、街頭與朋友感主題。 |
| Daido Moriyama（森山大道） | 噪訊街頭與焦躁城市語言 | `Inspired by Daido Moriyama, gritty high-contrast street photography with rough focus, heavy grain, harsh blacks, restless nocturnal city tension, raw fragmentary snapshot energy, underground urban unease` | 高反差、粗粒子、夜晚城市與焦躁感，適合巷口、地鐵、街邊與地下感場景。 |
| Nobuyoshi Araki（荒木經惟） | 私寫真與東京室內親密語言 | `Inspired by Nobuyoshi Araki, raw intimate diaristic portraiture, lived-in Tokyo interior mood, spontaneous flash-lit realism, private emotional tension, sensual domestic immediacy, candid personal snapshot atmosphere` | 私密、即興、貼近生活，帶東京室內與旅館感，適合臥室、老公寓、旅館與私人空間主題。 |
| Kishin Shinoyama（篠山紀信） | 經典寫真與名人肖像語言 | `Inspired by Kishin Shinoyama, polished Japanese gravure and celebrity portrait style, clean masterful control, glossy high-production finish, vivid feminine presence, sensual elegant charm, flattering studio lighting` | 乾淨、鮮明、人物魅力直接，兼具寫真與名人肖像感，適合棚拍、街拍與高完成度人物企劃。 |
| Chikashi Suzuki（鈴木親） | 年輕時尚雜誌與生活感語言 | `Inspired by Chikashi Suzuki, relaxed fashion editorial shot on film, youthful candid beauty with intuitive spontaneity, soft everyday realism, muted natural tones, clean Japanese magazine freshness, delicate film grain` | 年輕、自然、可親，帶日系時尚雜誌的清爽生活感，適合街頭、咖啡店、公寓與日常外景。 |
| Yuhki Toyama（奧山由之） | 青春電影感與透明敘事語言 | `Inspired by Yuhki Toyama, tender cinematic youth portraiture with quiet presence, fresh introspective realism, gentle diffused summer light, airy narrative stillness, translucent Japanese coming-of-age atmosphere, subtle melancholy` | 青春、透明、略帶電影感與情緒敘事，適合校園、街道、夏日戶外、天台與生活感人像。 |
| Leslie Kee（レスリー・キー） | 華麗明星感與高完成度商業語言 | `Inspired by Leslie Kee, polished star portrait photography with high-definition beauty lighting, immaculate skin rendering, iconic celebrity presence, vibrant commercial fashion finish, clean dramatic charisma, premium cover-shoot intensity` | 明星感強、商業完成度高、人物辨識度鮮明，適合棚拍、封面、紅毯感與高精修時裝主題。 |
| Eikoh Hosoe（細江英公） | 戲劇黑白與人體藝術張力語言 | `Inspired by Eikoh Hosoe, dramatic monochrome art portraiture with sculptural body tension, theatrical shadow contrast, stark chiaroscuro, surreal psychological intensity, avant-garde performance atmosphere, ritualistic visual force` | 黑白、戲劇、雕塑般肢體張力與藝術性很強，適合舞台光、極簡背景、人體藝術與概念黑白企劃。 |
| Guy Bourdin（蓋・布爾丁） | 鮮豔色塊與危險敘事時裝語言 | `Inspired by Guy Bourdin, bold narrative fashion portraiture with vivid color blocking, surreal tension, graphic contrast, dangerous glamour, editorial suspense, cinematic visual provocation` | 鮮豔色塊、強敘事張力與危險感，適合飯店、泳池、復古室內與概念時裝企劃。 |
| Miles Aldridge（邁爾斯・奧爾德里奇） | 濃烈色彩與復古電影棚拍語言 | `Inspired by Miles Aldridge, hyper-stylized studio portraiture with a saturated retro palette, elaborate set-piece design, glossy cinematic light, surreal domestic glamour, lacquered color precision` | 人工色彩很強、佈景精緻、帶復古未來感，適合棚拍、廚房、沙發、旅館與概念場景。 |
| Elsa Bleda（艾爾莎·布萊達） | 霓虹夜景與城市孤寂語言 | `Inspired by Elsa Bleda, nocturnal neon portraiture with moody urban silence, cinematic street glow, wet reflections, solitary city atmosphere, deep blue and magenta night tones, rain-soaked loneliness` | 夜晚城市、霓虹、濕地反光與孤獨感很強，適合巷口、街角、地鐵口與安靜夜街場景。 |
