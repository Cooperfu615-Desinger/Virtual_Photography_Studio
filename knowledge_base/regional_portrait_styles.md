# Photography Styles (現代人像攝影風格)

| 攝影風格 (Photography Style) | 靈感備註 (Inspiration Note) | 英文 Prompt 關鍵字 (Prompt Keywords) | 風格說明 (Style Description) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 清透寫真感 | — | `airy portrait photography, soft natural daylight, translucent skin tones, bright highlights, clean white atmosphere, gentle mood, soft focus background, realistic photography, delicate feminine presence` | 自然乾淨的日常人像，偏柔和亮調、空氣感與透明感，適合窗邊、臥室、白床景等明亮場景。 |
| 精緻棚拍感 | — | `clean studio portrait, polished beauty lighting, flawless skin rendering, glossy highlights, controlled shadows, luxury editorial atmosphere, professional fashion photography, refined feminine styling` | 乾淨俐落的棚拍成片語言，重視皮膚質感、精緻光線與商業感，適合時裝與美妝方向。 |
| 霓虹電影感 | — | `neon cinematic portrait, mixed artificial lighting, deep shadows, vivid red and blue highlights, wet street reflections, urban night mood, dramatic contrast, moody atmosphere, cinematic photography` | 夜晚都市、霓虹、人工混光與戲劇陰影，適合街頭、九龍、新宿、地下俱樂部等場景。 |
| 上田義彥 | 參考 Yoshihiko Ueda（上田義彥）類型的靜默自然人像語言 | `deep forest-like shadows, low-key lighting with dramatic chiaroscuro, muted cool color palette, side lighting carving delicate facial features, serene stoic expression, large format 8x10 film aesthetic, deep blacks, grainy texture, cinematic silence` | 深色陰影、低調光、冷淡色盤與靜默情緒，適合森林、窗邊、低光室內等安靜場景。 |
| 蜷川實花 | 參考 Mika Ninagawa（蜷川實花）類型的濃烈色彩與花卉戲劇感 | `hyper-saturated portrait photography, vivid floral surroundings, intense reds and pinks, luminous skin, dreamy theatrical color, glamorous feminine styling, glossy highlights, surreal romantic atmosphere, vibrant cinematic beauty` | 強烈飽和、鮮花、彩色光與夢境般張力，適合棚拍、花牆、彩色佈景與戲劇化寫真。 |
| 橫浪修 | 參考 Osamu Yokonami（橫浪修）類型的群像秩序與安靜疏離感 | `symmetrical portrait composition, quiet emotional distance, minimal gestures, spacious negative space, restrained color palette, still atmosphere, orderly visual rhythm, subtle documentary feeling, calm conceptual photography` | 強調排列、留白、安靜與輕微距離感，適合雙人構圖、空曠室內、校舍與極簡空間。 |
| 川內倫子 | 參考 Rinko Kawauchi（川內倫子）類型的輕盈日常與微光語言 | `poetic everyday portrait, soft ambient daylight, light-filled atmosphere, delicate highlights, airy stillness, intimate daily moments, pastel softness, subtle emotional warmth, lyrical realism` | 輕盈、柔光、日常碎片與微過曝感，適合窗邊、居家、草地與安靜生活場景。 |
| 柔霧古典夢境感 | 參考 Paolo Roversi（保羅・羅韋爾西）類型的柔霧高級時裝語言 | `soft haze fashion portrait, dim romantic lighting, velvety shadows, painterly softness, muted elegant tones, fragile beauty, dreamy editorial atmosphere, timeless femininity, poetic studio image` | 柔霧、低速感、暗色調與古典脆弱感，適合棚拍、復古室內與高級時裝人像。 |
| 極簡雕塑棚拍感 | 參考 Irving Penn（歐文・佩恩）類型的極簡背景與雕塑式姿態 | `minimal studio portrait, sculptural pose, clean backdrop, precise composition, crisp tonal contrast, elegant restraint, timeless fashion image, sharp structure, refined stillness` | 乾淨背景、雕塑感姿態與精準構圖，適合純背景棚拍、時裝封面與肖像企劃。 |
| 冷冽權力性感 | 參考 Helmut Newton（赫爾穆特・牛頓）類型的冷感奢華與強勢姿態 | `cold glamorous fashion portrait, assertive pose, luxury urban mood, sharp contrast, powerful feminine presence, sleek styling, sensual authority, bold editorial energy, cinematic tension` | 冷感、權力關係、都市奢華與強勢性感，適合飯店、電梯、夜晚室內與高級時裝主題。 |
| 俏皮性感雜誌感 | 參考 Ellen von Unwerth（艾倫・馮・昂沃斯）類型的 playful 性感與抓拍雜誌語言 | `playful sensual editorial portrait, spontaneous energy, feminine confidence, lively magazine photography, candid movement, flirtatious mood, flash-assisted fashion image, spirited glamour, cheeky allure` | 俏皮、性感、抓拍感強，介於雜誌與寫真之間，適合床景、沙發、街頭與隨拍主題。 |
| 私密生活紀錄感 | 參考 Nan Goldin（南・戈爾丁）類型的私人相簿與粗粒子室內語言 | `intimate documentary portrait, warm indoor light, raw emotional atmosphere, grainy texture, personal snapshot feeling, imperfect realism, private room ambience, lived-in details, emotional closeness` | 私密、粗粒子、暖光與生活混亂感，適合臥室、旅館、洗手台鏡前等私人空間。 |
| 粗糙直閃時尚感 | 參考 Juergen Teller（尤爾根・特勒）類型的直閃粗糙與反精緻時裝語言 | `direct flash fashion portrait, deliberately raw styling, anti-polished editorial mood, flat flash lighting, awkward realism, unretouched skin texture, immediate camera presence, casual luxury tension` | 直打閃光、不完美與反精緻時尚感，適合白牆、電梯、街頭、試衣間與社群式大片。 |
| 純背景凝視肖像感 | 參考 Richard Avedon（理察・阿維頓）類型的純背景與直接凝視語言 | `clean backdrop portrait, direct eye contact, sharp emotional presence, minimal studio setting, crisp tonal contrast, iconic fashion portraiture, stripped-down composition, commanding gaze, timeless intensity` | 純背景、直接凝視與人物存在感極強，適合棚拍、封面感肖像與高辨識度人物企劃。 |
| 空曠美式紀實感 | 參考 Alec Soth（亞歷克・索斯）類型的寬鬆構圖與平靜敘事語言 | `quiet documentary portrait, spacious composition, subtle melancholy, everyday realism, soft natural light, open landscape feeling, calm emotional distance, understated storytelling, American road atmosphere` | 構圖留白較多、氣氛平靜、帶有孤獨與旅行感，適合郊區、公路、旅館與空曠戶外場景。 |
| 古典濕版記憶感 | 參考 Sally Mann（莎莉・曼）類型的古典濕版與記憶感影像語言 | `wet plate inspired portrait, antique tonal range, soft blur, ghostly texture, timeworn atmosphere, poetic decay, delicate monochrome mood, nostalgic stillness, memory-like image` | 古典、朦朧、帶有時間感與衰敗氣息，適合森林、老屋、復古室內與黑白低調人像。 |
| 青春日常隨拍感 | 參考 Wolfgang Tillmans（沃夫岡・提爾曼斯）類型的生活切片與非正式肖像語言 | `casual youthful portrait, everyday snapshot energy, unforced pose, natural light realism, spontaneous composition, contemporary lifestyle mood, intimate yet relaxed atmosphere, subtle art-documentary tone` | 生活切片、輕鬆隨拍與非正式構圖，適合公寓、咖啡店、街頭與朋友感主題。 |
| 高反差黑白街頭感 | 參考 Daido Moriyama（森山大道）類型的噪訊街頭與焦躁城市語言 | `high contrast black and white street portrait, gritty grain, rough focus, urban night tension, raw snapshot energy, noisy texture, restless city mood, stark shadows, rebellious documentary feeling` | 高反差黑白、粗粒子、夜晚城市與焦躁感，適合巷口、地鐵、街邊與地下感場景。 |
| 危險敘事時尚感 | 參考 Guy Bourdin（蓋・布爾丁）類型的鮮豔色塊與危險敘事時裝語言 | `bold narrative fashion portrait, vivid color blocking, provocative composition, surreal tension, luxurious styling, cinematic fashion drama, sharp graphic contrast, enigmatic glamour, editorial suspense` | 鮮豔色塊、強敘事張力與危險感，適合飯店、泳池、復古室內與概念時裝企劃。 |
| 濃彩復古電影棚拍感 | 參考 Miles Aldridge（邁爾斯・奧爾德里奇）類型的濃烈色彩與復古電影棚拍語言 | `hyper-stylized fashion portrait, saturated retro color palette, elaborate set design, glossy cinematic lighting, theatrical femininity, surreal domestic glamour, polished magazine image, vivid artificial tones` | 人工色彩很強、佈景精緻、帶復古未來感，適合棚拍、廚房、沙發、旅館與概念場景。 |
