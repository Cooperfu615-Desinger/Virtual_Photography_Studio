# Photography Styles (現代人像攝影風格)

| 攝影風格 (Photography Style) | 靈感備註 (Inspiration Note) | 英文 Prompt 關鍵字 (Prompt Keywords) | 風格說明 (Style Description) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 清透寫真感 | — | `airy portrait photography, soft natural daylight, translucent skin tones, bright highlights, clean white atmosphere, gentle mood, soft focus background, realistic photography, delicate feminine presence` | 自然乾淨的日常人像，偏柔和亮調、空氣感與透明感，適合窗邊、臥室、白床景等明亮場景。 |
| 精緻棚拍感 | — | `clean studio portrait, polished beauty lighting, flawless skin rendering, glossy highlights, controlled shadows, luxury editorial atmosphere, professional fashion photography, refined feminine styling` | 乾淨俐落的棚拍成片語言，重視皮膚質感、精緻光線與商業感，適合時裝與美妝方向。 |
| 霓虹電影感 | — | `neon cinematic portrait, mixed artificial lighting, deep shadows, vivid red and blue highlights, wet street reflections, urban night mood, dramatic contrast, moody atmosphere, cinematic photography` | 夜晚都市、霓虹、人工混光與戲劇陰影，適合街頭、九龍、新宿、地下俱樂部等場景。 |
| 靜謐森林低調電影感 | 參考 Yoshihiko Ueda 類型的靜默自然人像語言 | `deep forest-like shadows, low-key lighting with dramatic chiaroscuro, muted cool color palette, side lighting carving delicate facial features, serene stoic expression, large format 8x10 film aesthetic, deep blacks, grainy texture, cinematic silence` | 深色陰影、低調光、冷淡色盤與靜默情緒，適合森林、窗邊、低光室內等安靜場景。 |
| 高飽和花卉夢境感 | 參考 Mika Ninagawa 類型的濃烈色彩與花卉戲劇感 | `hyper-saturated portrait photography, vivid floral surroundings, intense reds and pinks, luminous skin, dreamy theatrical color, glamorous feminine styling, glossy highlights, surreal romantic atmosphere, vibrant cinematic beauty` | 強烈飽和、鮮花、彩色光與夢境般張力，適合棚拍、花牆、彩色佈景與戲劇化寫真。 |
| 對稱留白疏離感 | 參考 Osamu Yokonami 類型的群像秩序與安靜疏離感 | `symmetrical portrait composition, quiet emotional distance, minimal gestures, spacious negative space, restrained color palette, still atmosphere, orderly visual rhythm, subtle documentary feeling, calm conceptual photography` | 強調排列、留白、安靜與輕微距離感，適合雙人構圖、空曠室內、校舍與極簡空間。 |
